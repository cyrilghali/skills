# Lending

## Terms

**Member**: a person registered with the library who may borrow books.

**Loan**: one copy of a book checked out by a Member, due back after 21 days. A Member holds at most 3 active Loans.

**Reservation**: a Member's place in the queue for a book whose every copy is currently on Loan. When a copy comes back, the first Reservation is notified.

**Renewal**: extending an active Loan's due date by 21 days. A Loan with a pending Reservation on its book cannot be renewed.
