# Brief: u02-the-sorting-line — How the machines sort your recycling

- **Series goal:** residents know what happens to their blue bin, and so why some items do not belong in it.
- **Audience — resident:** anyone who lives in the city and uses a blue bin. Already knows: what goes in the blue bin.
- **Tier:** overview (1 of 1); stands alone: yes
- **The one idea:** the sorting plant separates mixed recycling by what each material does near a machine.
- **Length:** 30 seconds, for a social media slot
- **Prerequisites:** none
