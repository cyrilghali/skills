# Crumb & Co. — closing the front counter

One page, pinned by the till. For counter staff on the evening shift.

1. Flip the door sign to "Closed" at 18:00 sharp, even if someone is still inside.
2. Serve anyone already inside; let no one new in.
3. Unsold bread goes into the paper bags marked "Food bank". Pastries with cream go in the bin, never in the bags.
4. Write the number of food-bank bags on the clipboard by the back door. The driver collects at 18:30.
5. Wipe the display cases with the blue cloths and the spray from the green bottle. The red cloths are for the toilets only.
6. Turn the display fridge to night mode with the button on its left side. Do not switch it off.
7. Count the cash drawer. Leave 150 euros in coins and small notes as the float for tomorrow.
8. Put the rest of the cash in the grey bag, write the total on the slip, and drop it in the floor safe.
9. Print the card terminal's end-of-day report and staple it to the cash slip copy.
10. If the cash total and the till report differ by more than 5 euros, text the manager before you leave.
11. Sweep, then mop from the back of the shop toward the door.
12. Empty the bins into the yard container. Lock the yard gate.
13. Turn off the lights with the switch panel behind the counter; the night light over the safe stays on.
14. Set the alarm with your personal code. You have 45 seconds to leave.
15. Lock the front door with both locks.

Note from the manager: an older version of this sheet said to leave 200 euros as the float. That is outdated since we moved to the smaller till. The float is 150 euros.
