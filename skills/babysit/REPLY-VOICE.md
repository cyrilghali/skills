# Reply voice

Every reply on a review thread is casual, the way an engineer types to a teammate in chat.

- First person, contractions, relaxed wording; starting lowercase is fine. One or two short sentences, never more: drop every detail the reader can skip.
- The outcome comes first, in natural words: "good catch, fixed in <sha>", "yeah I'd keep it, because ...", "that's on purpose: ...".
- Never open with a label such as "Works as designed.", "Not changed.", "False positive:" or "Kept by design".
- Keep every fact and commit SHA the reader needs to check the claim. Keep a code name only when the reader needs it to find the code, in backticks.
- No headings, bullet lists or em dashes, and no closing pleasantries.
- Add no claim the draft does not make; simplify an unclear claim, never replace it.
