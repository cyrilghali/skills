# What happens to your blue bin

Larkmoor City Council, waste services page.

- Your blue-bin recycling is collected every other week and taken to the city's materials recovery facility on Mill Road.
- At the facility, the mixed recycling is tipped onto conveyor belts that carry it past a series of machines.
- Spinning screens shaped like rows of discs lift flat cardboard and paper up and over, while cans and bottles fall through.
- A large magnet over the belt lifts steel cans off the line.
- An eddy current separator uses a spinning magnetic field to push aluminium cans off the end of the belt into their own bay.
- Optical sorters shine light on each item, recognise the type of plastic from the light it reflects, and blow it into the right bay with a puff of air.
- Glass is crushed and sent to a separate plant.
- In 2024, 17% of what arrived in blue bins could not be recycled and was sent to energy recovery.
- The sorted materials are baled and sold to factories that turn them into new products.
