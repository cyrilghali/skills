# Episode 3 source: how a returned book gets back to the shelf

Written by the head librarian for the volunteer onboarding series.

1. Returns go through the book drop by the front door, open 24 hours a day.
2. The book drop is emptied three times a day: at opening, at noon and at closing.
3. Each returned book is scanned at the returns desk; the scan clears it from the borrower's account.
4. A scanned book with a hold on it goes to the holds shelf instead of back into the stacks.
5. The holder gets an email, and the book waits on the holds shelf for 7 days.
6. A book that is not picked up in 7 days goes to the next person on the hold list, or back to the stacks if there is none.
7. Every returned book is checked for damage before it goes back out.
8. A damaged book goes to the repair cart; the volunteer coordinator decides on repair or withdrawal each Friday.
9. Books with no hold go onto a sorting cart, grouped by floor.
10. Fiction is shelved alphabetically by the author's last name.
11. Non-fiction is shelved by Dewey Decimal number, from 000 to 999.
12. Volunteers reshelve each cart within 24 hours of the return.
13. Before reshelving, a volunteer reads the shelf around the gap to catch a misplaced book.
14. A book shelved in the wrong place counts as lost until someone finds it, because the catalogue sends readers to its proper spot.
