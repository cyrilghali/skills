# Brief: u03-memory — Why the second time is different

- **Series goal:** a viewer with no biology training can explain in their own words what a vaccine does in the body.
- **Audience — general public:** adults who watch the public-health channel, no biology training. Already knows: the overview units.
- **Tier:** deep-dive (2 of 2); stands alone: no, it builds on its prerequisites
- **The one idea:** a vaccine leaves memory cells behind, so a later infection is met faster and harder than the first time.
- **Length:** about 2 minutes
- **Prerequisites:** u01-what-a-vaccine-does (a vaccine shows the body a harmless piece of a germ), u02-antibodies (B cells make antibodies that latch onto one antigen)
